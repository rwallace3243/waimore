How WaiMore works

Five steps, one loop. Assess. Learn. Build. Demonstrate. Advance. Then the loop runs again — at a higher level.

01 · ASSESS

Know where you stand

Take your AI & Career baseline fluency assessment — skills, gaps, job risk, and your personal Career GPS.

02 · LEARN

Micro-learnings matched to your gaps, spoon-fed by Coach Luma™ — direction, not another course catalog.

03 · BUILD

Real problems submitted by real members become guided team builds — prompts, workflows, working solutions.

04 · DEMONSTRATE

Earn proof, not points

Badges tied to what you actually built — portfolio artifacts and capability proof, not vanity gamification.

05 · ADVANCE

Move up on purpose

Your roadmap updates, your level rises, and new rooms open — all the way to the S4 Advisor Circle.

This is not a forum

WaiMore runs on the Challenge-to-Solution flywheel. Every member problem makes the whole community smarter.

The Quest flywheel

Your unsolved problem is the community's next build. Here's the loop:

SUBMIT

Any member drops a real AI problem in the Suggestion Box — free.

SELECT

Advisor Circle members vote on which problems become official Quests.

BUILD

Builder teams solve it in guided sessions — workshops, prompts, workflows.

SHIP

Outputs become playbooks, prompt packs, and assets in the member library.

LEARN

Patterns feed community intelligence reports — and what gets built next.

Every tier has a role: Free members supply the signal. Builders solve. Advisors steer. Everyone advances.
